Accounts

Every sign-in account in Firebase, checked every 30 minutes, with the devices each has signed in from. Pick PlutoScan to see its accounts and who has paid.

Written by the security functions in this repo (see the README). Sign-ins, new accounts, emails and SMS codes are recorded by Firebase itself. Failed and refused sign-ins are sent by each app, so a person calling Firebase directly can skip them; Identity Platform's own logs in Google Cloud are the backup. Very busy data (live driver positions, API counters, the apps' own history logs) is not watched, to keep costs down. Entries are kept for 90 days, alerts for a year.
